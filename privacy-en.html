Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Rantideb's Blog ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our website at https://textandtech.me/.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information: When you visit our website, we and third-party services may automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • IP address (approximate geographic location)
  • Pages you visit and time spent on pages
  • Referring website/source
  • Device identifiers and cookies
How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on our site, analyze site traffic, and serve personalized advertisements. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function (e.g., remembering your cookie consent preference).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (via Google Analytics or similar services).
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense and other ad networks to serve personalized ads based on your browsing behavior.
Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect and process your personal data:

  • Google AdSense : Google AdSense serves ads on our site and may use cookies and similar technologies to personalize ads and measure ad performance. For more information, see Google's Advertising Policies and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand site usage and improve our content. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase: We use Firebase for comments and real-time database features. Firebase may collect device and usage data. See Firebase Privacy.
Your Rights and Choices

Cookie Consent: When you first visit our site, you will see a cookie consent banner. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies. Your choice is stored locally in your browser.

Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) Opt-Out

Your Data Protection Rights (EU/EEA): If you are in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification (correction) of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing (including direct marketing and profiling)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email below.

California Residents (CCPA): If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your personal information, and opt out of the "sale" of personal information (note: we do not sell personal data, but third-party ad networks may share data for targeted advertising, which can be considered a "sale" under CCPA). You can opt out via the links above.

Data Retention

We retain automatically collected data (via cookies and analytics) for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Cookie data is typically retained for up to 26 months (Google Analytics default). You can clear cookies from your browser at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
